# DPANet datasets

Publicly available datasets for fire and smoke detection are listed below.

## Public datasets

1. D-Fire

   The D-Fire dataset is available at https://github.com/gaia-solutions-on-demand/DFireDataset and is detailed in the literature:

   De Venâncio, P. V. A., Rezende, T. M., Lisboa, A. C., & Barbosa, A. V. (2021, November). Fire detection based on a two-dimensional convolutional neural network and temporal analysis. In 2021 IEEE Latin American Conference on Computational Intelligence (LA-CCI) (pp. 1–6). IEEE.

2. FLAME

   The FLAME dataset can be accessed at https://github.com/AlirezaShamsoshoara/Fire-Detection-UAV-Aerial-Image-Classification-Segmentation-UnmannedAerialVehicle and is detailed in the literature:

   Shamsoshoara, A., Afghah, F., Razi, A., Zheng, L., Fulé, P. Z., & Blasch, E. (2021). Aerial imagery pile burn detection using deep learning: The FLAME dataset. Computer Networks, 193, 108001.

3. M4SFWD

   The M4SFWD dataset is available at https://github.com/Philharmy-Wang/M4SFWD and is detailed in the literature:

   Wang, G., Li, H., Li, P., Lang, X., Feng, Y., Ding, Z., & Xie, S. (2024). M4SFWD: A Multi-Faceted synthetic dataset for remote sensing forest wildfires detection. Expert Systems with Applications, 248, 123489.

4. Yuxi Wildfire

   The Yuxi Wildfire dataset is available at https://github.com/Philharmy-Wang/yuxifire and is detailed in the literature:

   Wang, G., Li, H., Ye, S., Zhao, H., Ding, H., & Xie, S. (2024). RFWNet: A multiscale remote sensing forest wildfire detection network with digital twinning, adaptive spatial aggregation, and dynamic sparse features. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 62, 1–23.

## FLAME bounding-box annotations

The download below provides manual bounding-box annotations for 2,003 images from the FLAME dataset, covering fire and smoke. The annotations were prepared for our DPANet study and are publicly available to support reproducible research on UAV-based forest fire detection.
